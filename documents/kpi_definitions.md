# KPI Definitions

**Document type:** Business Definitions
**Owner:** Analytics Team
**Status:** Draft v1 (project-authored, synthetic)
**Last updated:** 2026-09-08

This document defines the official business metrics used across all analytics,
reporting, and agent-generated investigations. If a metric is not defined here,
it is not an approved KPI and should not be reported as one without flagging
that it is ad-hoc.

---

## Active Customer

A customer is considered **active** if they have placed at least one order with
a status of `delivered` within the **trailing 180 days** from the reporting
date.

- The 180-day window is measured from `orders.order_delivered_customer_date`,
  not `order_purchase_timestamp` — we count from when the customer actually
  received value, not when they clicked buy.
- Customers must be resolved to their `customer_unique_id` (via the customer
  crosswalk) before this rule is applied. A customer who ordered under two
  different `customer_id` values is still one active customer, not two.
- Orders with status `canceled` or `unavailable` do not count toward activity,
  even if a delivery date is present in error.

**Non-active** customers are anyone who has ordered before but falls outside
the 180-day window. This document does not define "churned" (see below).

## Churned Customer

A customer is **churned** if they were active at some point in the past but
have had no delivered order in the trailing 365 days. Churn is evaluated on a
rolling basis, not a fixed cohort — a customer can move out of churned status
by placing a new order.

## Revenue

**Revenue** for a given period is the sum of `order_items.price` (excluding
freight/shipping charges) for orders with status `delivered`, attributed to
the order's `order_delivered_customer_date`.

- Freight (`order_items.freight_value`) is tracked separately as **Shipping
  Revenue** and is not included in the headline Revenue figure unless
  explicitly labeled "Revenue incl. freight."
- Revenue is attributed by delivery date, not purchase date, for consistency
  with the Active Customer definition above. A "Q3 revenue" figure means
  orders delivered in Q3, not ordered in Q3.
- Orders that are `canceled` after payment but before delivery are excluded
  entirely, including any partial payments captured.

## Average Order Value (AOV)

AOV = Revenue (as defined above) / count of distinct `order_id` values in the
same period, for delivered orders only.

## Return Rate

Return Rate for a given product, seller, or region is:

```
returns count / order_items count (delivered)
```

over the same period and same grouping (product/seller/region). A return rate
is only meaningful with a denominator of at least 20 delivered order items —
smaller sample sizes should be reported as "insufficient volume," not as a
misleadingly precise percentage.

An "unusually high" return rate (as referenced in ad-hoc investigation
questions) means a rate more than **2 standard deviations above the mean
return rate** for its comparison group (e.g. a product compared against other
products in the same category, not against the global average).

## Repeat Purchase Rate

Share of active customers (per the definition above) who have placed **more
than one** delivered order, ever, as of the reporting date. This is a
lifetime measure, not limited to the 180-day active window — a customer can
be newly active but not yet a repeat purchaser.

## Regional Contribution

The percentage share of total Revenue attributable to a region, where a
seller's region is derived via `sellers → employees → regions` (never stored
directly on `sellers`) and a customer's region is derived via the customer's
delivery address state, mapped through `state_region_map`. When a
region-level metric mixes seller-side and customer-side attribution, the
report must state explicitly which side is being measured — revenue
"generated by" a region (seller-side) and revenue "delivered to" a region
(customer-side) are different numbers and are not interchangeable.

# Seller Performance Policy

**Document type:** Business Policy  
**Owner:** Marketplace Operations  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective date:** January 2018  
**Scope:** All sellers registered on the Olist platform

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the performance standards that all Olist marketplace
sellers must meet to maintain active listing status. It establishes the
metrics used to evaluate seller health, the thresholds that trigger
intervention, and the process for suspension or removal from the platform.

---

## 2. Key Performance Indicators

Every active seller is evaluated monthly across four dimensions:

### 2.1 Order Fulfilment Rate

**Definition:** The percentage of confirmed orders that are dispatched
within the promised handling time window.

**Threshold:** Sellers must maintain a fulfilment rate of at least **95%**
over any rolling 30-day window.

**Measurement:** An order is considered fulfilled on time if the carrier
scan timestamp falls within the seller's declared handling time plus one
business day of grace.

### 2.2 Return Rate

**Definition:** The number of customer-initiated return requests divided
by total delivered orders, expressed as a percentage.

**Threshold:** Sellers must maintain a return rate **below 5%** over any
rolling 90-day window.

**High-risk categories:** Sellers in the Watches & Gifts and Computers &
Accessories categories are subject to a tighter threshold of **4%** due to
historically elevated return rates in those categories.

**Consequence:** Sellers exceeding 5% return rate for two consecutive
90-day windows are placed on a Return Rate Improvement Plan (RRIP). A
third consecutive breach triggers automatic listing suspension pending
review.

### 2.3 Review Score

**Definition:** The average customer review score across all reviewed
orders in a rolling 90-day window, on a scale of 1 to 5.

**Threshold:** Sellers must maintain an average review score of at least
**3.8 out of 5.0**.

**Minimum reviews required:** This metric is only applied once a seller
has received at least 10 reviews in the measurement window. New sellers
with fewer than 10 reviews are evaluated against a provisional standard
of 3.5.

### 2.4 Response Time

**Definition:** The average time in hours between a customer message and
the seller's first response, measured during business hours (09:00–18:00
local time, Monday–Friday).

**Threshold:** Average response time must remain **below 24 hours** over
any rolling 30-day window.

---

## 3. Seller Tiers

Sellers are classified into three tiers based on their combined
performance score, recalculated monthly:

| Tier | Name | Criteria |
|------|------|----------|
| 1 | Platinum | All four KPIs exceed threshold by 20% or more |
| 2 | Standard | All four KPIs meet or exceed threshold |
| 3 | At-Risk | One or more KPIs below threshold |

**Platinum sellers** receive: priority listing placement, reduced
commission rate of 12% (vs standard 15%), access to promotional campaign
slots, and a dedicated account manager.

**At-Risk sellers** receive: a written performance notice within 5
business days of tier classification, a 60-day improvement window, and
mandatory participation in the Seller Success programme.

---

## 4. Suspension and Removal

### 4.1 Automatic Suspension

A seller's listings are automatically suspended (all products set to
inactive, no new orders accepted) under any of the following conditions:

- Return rate exceeds **8%** in any single 30-day window
- Review score falls below **2.5** in any rolling 30-day window with at
  least 5 reviews
- Fulfilment rate falls below **80%** in any 14-day window
- Two or more active customer fraud reports confirmed by Olist Trust &
  Safety

Suspension takes effect within 24 hours of the triggering condition
being confirmed by the automated monitoring system.

### 4.2 Permanent Removal

Permanent removal from the platform is applied when:

- A seller has been suspended three or more times in a 12-month period
- A confirmed case of product counterfeiting or fraud
- Violation of Olist's prohibited items policy
- Failure to respond to a suspension notice within 30 calendar days

### 4.3 Appeals

Suspended sellers may file an appeal within 14 calendar days of
suspension notification. Appeals are reviewed by the Seller Trust
Committee within 10 business days. The committee may reinstate the
seller, extend the suspension, or convert to permanent removal.

---

## 5. Monitoring and Reporting

Seller performance dashboards are updated daily. Sellers can access
their own KPI dashboard through the Seller Portal at any time.

Monthly performance reports are generated on the first business day of
each month covering the prior calendar month. Sellers whose metrics show
a declining trend across three consecutive months receive an early warning
notification even if thresholds have not yet been breached.

The Marketplace Operations team reviews aggregate seller performance
weekly and escalates anomalies — such as a sudden spike in return rates
across a product category — to the relevant category management team
within 48 hours.

---

## 6. Definitions

**Active seller:** A seller with at least one listing in published status
and at least one order fulfilled in the past 90 days.

**Handling time:** The period in calendar days between order confirmation
and carrier pickup scan, as declared by the seller at listing creation.

**Return request:** A customer-initiated contact requesting to return a
delivered item, regardless of whether the return is ultimately approved.
Initiated returns are counted even if the seller successfully resolves
the issue without a physical return.

**Rolling window:** A measurement period that ends on the current date and
extends backwards by the specified number of days, recalculated daily.
It is not a fixed calendar month unless otherwise specified.
